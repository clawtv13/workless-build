## 4. REGULATORY LANDSCAPE (AI ACT)

### EU AI Act — Key Points for Spain:

- **Enacted:** June 2024 (Reglamento UE 2024/1689)
- **Risk-based approach:** Higher impact = more obligations
- **Spain's role:** Agencia Española de Supervisión de IA (created Aug 2023)

### Risk Categories:
1. **Prohibited** (unacceptable risk): Real-time biometric surveillance in public (with exceptions)
2. **High-risk**: Employment systems, credit scoring, critical infrastructure
3. **Limited risk**: Transparency obligations (chatbots must disclose they're AI)
4. **Minimal risk**: Most applications

### Impact on Spanish Companies:

- **49%** cite regulation/governance as main barrier to GenAI adoption (Deloitte Spain)
- **70%** highly concerned about proprietary data use
- **80%** report increased trust in AI since GenAI emergence (vs 2 years ago)

### Compliance Requirements:
- Documentation (Annex IV, simplified for SMEs)
- Risk assessments for high-risk systems
- Human oversight mechanisms
- Transparent AI disclosure

### Spain-Specific:
- Real Decreto 817/2023: Sandbox for AI compliance testing
- Real Decreto 729/2023: AESIA statute (supervision agency)

**Sources:**
- BOE (AI Act, AESIA decree)
- Deloitte State of AI 2026 (Spain)
- MBIT School AI compliance guide
- BBVA, UNIA regulatory analysis
