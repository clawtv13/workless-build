## PART 3: MARCO REGULATORIO (1,500 words)

### 3.1 AI Act: Qué Cambia para las Empresas Españolas (600 words)
**Keyword:** regulación ia españa ai act

- **Timeline:**
  - Enacted: June 2024 (Reglamento UE 2024/1689)
  - Phased implementation: 2024-2027
  - Full enforcement: 2026-2027

- **Risk-based framework explained:**
  - **Prohibited** (red): Real-time biometrics, social scoring
  - **High-risk** (orange): HR systems, credit scoring, critical infrastructure
  - **Limited risk** (yellow): Chatbots (must disclose AI)
  - **Minimal risk** (green): Most applications

- **Spain's enforcement:**
  - AESIA (Agencia Española de Supervisión de IA) - created Aug 2023
  - Sandbox program (Real Decreto 817/2023) for compliance testing

- **Impact data:**
  - 49% cite regulation as barrier to GenAI adoption
  - 70% highly concerned about proprietary data
  - 80% increased trust in AI (since GenAI emergence)

- **What Spanish companies must do:**
  1. Classify AI systems by risk level
  2. Document high-risk systems (Annex IV)
  3. Implement human oversight
  4. Disclose AI use (transparency)
  5. Prepare for audits
