## [01:03-01:18] SEO KEYWORD STRATEGY UPDATE

**Key insight from n0mad:** Generic keywords ("IA España") are impossible to rank. Focus on long-tail, specific keywords.

**Framework added to AUTHORITY-STRATEGY.md:**

**Avoid (too competitive):**
- ❌ "IA España" (impossible)
- ❌ "ChatGPT" (too broad)
- ❌ "Inteligencia artificial 2026"

**Target (achievable, high-value):**
- ✅ "mejor herramienta IA ecommerce español"
- ✅ "cuanto cuesta implementar IA pyme"
- ✅ "chatgpt vs claude español comparativa"
- ✅ "automatizar atención cliente whatsapp IA"
- ✅ "roi inteligencia artificial retail españa"

**Keyword research workflow (MANDATORY for every piece):**
1. Identify 20-30 seed keywords (specific problems/use cases)
2. Filter by sweet spot:
   - Search volume: 100-5,000/month
   - Keyword difficulty: 0-30 (achievable)
   - Intent: commercial > informational
3. Cluster 10-15 keywords per post
4. Map to content structure (each H2 = keyword variation)

**Result:** Each post ranks for 10-15 specific searches vs 0 for generic terms.

**Impact:** 10,000 visits/month (position #1-5 in 15 keywords) vs 1,000 visits/month (position #50 in 1 keyword).
